# Customer Qualification Criteria

## Purpose

Determine whether a company has sufficient operational and economic fit for the AI Revenue Recovery & Conversion System.

The qualification process should rely on measurable business information rather than unsupported AI assumptions.

---

## Primary Signals

### 1. Inbound Lead Volume

Measure:

- Total inbound leads
- Leads per day
- Leads per week
- Leads per month

---

### 2. Missed Opportunities

Measure:

- Missed calls
- Unanswered calls
- Unanswered SMS
- Unanswered emails
- After-hours inquiries

---

### 3. Follow-Up Leakage

Measure:

- Leads without follow-up
- Qualified leads without booking
- Leads that stopped responding
- Old leads without reactivation attempts

---

### 4. Appointment Leakage

Measure:

- Cancellations
- No-shows
- Rescheduling requests
- Appointments not confirmed

---

### 5. Average Job Value

Measure:

- Average completed job revenue
- Relevant service-specific job value where available

---

### 6. Recoverable Revenue

Estimate using observable historical opportunities.

Conceptually:

Recoverable Revenue
=
Recoverable Opportunities
×
Estimated Conversion
×
Average Job Value

The system must distinguish measured values from estimates.

---

### 7. Operational Capacity

Evaluate:

- Technician capacity
- Service area
- Available appointment capacity
- Emergency availability
- Business operating hours

A company should not be considered economically suitable solely because it has many leads if it cannot operationally serve additional jobs.

---

### 8. Required Integrations

Check availability of:

- Phone
- SMS
- Email
- CRM
- Calendar / Scheduling

---

## Qualification Principle

Company qualification should ultimately evaluate:

Lead Volume
→ Opportunity Leakage
→ Recoverable Revenue
→ Operational Capacity
→ ROI Potential

---

## Important Rule

AI may analyze available information.

AI must not invent missing business data.

Unknown values remain unknown until obtained from the customer, integration, or verified data source.
