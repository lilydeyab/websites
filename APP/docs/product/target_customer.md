# Target Customer

## Product

AI Revenue Recovery & Conversion System for Home-Service Companies.

## Primary Market

U.S. home-service companies.

## Initial Vertical

Plumbing, Heating, and Air-Conditioning contractors.

## Core Customer Characteristics

The target company typically has:

- Incoming phone calls
- Incoming leads
- Customer conversations
- Appointments
- Technicians
- Jobs
- Revenue-generating service operations
- A scheduling workflow
- A CRM or customer-management workflow
- A meaningful volume of inbound opportunities

## Core Business Problem

Revenue leakage across the customer lifecycle.

The system focuses on opportunities that may be lost between:

Lead
→ Conversation
→ Qualification
→ Appointment
→ Job
→ Revenue

## Economic-Fit Signals

The system should evaluate economic fit using measurable business signals.

Primary signals:

- Inbound lead volume
- Missed calls
- After-hours leads
- Unanswered messages
- Unbooked qualified leads
- Follow-up gaps
- Appointment cancellations
- No-shows
- Old leads
- Average job value
- Service area
- Operational capacity
- CRM availability
- Calendar availability

## Economic-Fit Principle

The system must not assume that company size alone determines economic fit.

Economic fit should be evaluated using observable signals such as:

Lead Volume
→ Missed / Unhandled Opportunities
→ Recoverable Revenue
→ Operational Capacity
→ System ROI Potential

## Required Infrastructure

A suitable customer should have or be able to provide access to relevant operational systems, including where applicable:

- Phone communication
- SMS
- Email
- Calendar / scheduling
- CRM
- Customer and lead information

## Exclusion / Review Conditions

A company may require additional review when:

- Lead volume is too low to justify the system
- Recoverable revenue appears insufficient
- Required integrations are unavailable
- Operational capacity cannot support additional jobs
- Business rules require human handling
- The company operates outside the supported service model

## Market Sizing Reference

Initial market reference:

- NAICS: 238220
- U.S. establishments in 2023: 111,207
- Establishments with 20+ employees: 12,601
- Establishments with 50+ employees: 4,217

The 4,217 figure is a conservative premium proxy, not the actual number of companies willing or able to pay $3,000+ per month.

The actual economic-fit segment must be determined through qualification.

## Product Positioning

The product is positioned as:

AI Revenue Recovery & Conversion System

It is not positioned as only:

- An AI chatbot
- An AI receptionist
- A CRM
- A booking application

The product connects these capabilities into a revenue-recovery pipeline.
